Crime Analysis

An exploratory analysis of the UCI Communities and Crime dataset — 1,994 US communities, 128 socio-economic and law-enforcement attributes, one target: violent crimes per population. This page renders the cleaned dataset's key metrics directly from the analysis pipeline in the repository.

Key findings

Strongest protective factors

  • PctKids2Par — % of families with kids headed by two parents (r = −0.74)
  • PctFam2Par — % of kids in two-parent family housing (r = −0.71)
  • racePctWhite — % of population that is Caucasian (r = −0.68)
  • PctYoungKids2Par / PctTeen2Par — two-parent households by child age band (r ≈ −0.66)

Strongest risk factors

  • PctIlleg — % of illegitimate births (r = 0.74)
  • racepctblack — % of population that is Black (r = 0.63)
  • pctWPubAsst — % of households with public assistance income (r = 0.57)
  • FemalePctDiv / TotalPctDiv — divorce rates (r ≈ 0.55)

Interpret cautiously: many of these variables are proxies for poverty and family structure rather than independent causal factors, and the dataset carries known sampling and reporting biases (see the UCI dataset documentation).
